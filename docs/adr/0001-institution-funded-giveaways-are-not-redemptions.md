# Institution-funded giveaways are not redemptions

The 17 June 2026 night event gave away 215 free `Student Meal` / `Student
Drink` items and 68 `TUSA After Dark` items, all paid for by TUSA. They were
first counted as redemptions on the reasoning that "somebody else paid". We
decided they are not. A redemption must draw down a donation from the public,
and TUSA covering an event is institutional funding, not a donation. Counting
them made June look like a boom and every later month look like a collapse.
They made up 173 of the 246 published redemption rows, about 70%, which
overstated the published impact figures.

## Consequences

The public `/grounded` figures fall when this is applied, and the widget will
show lower numbers than it did before. That is the correction, not a
regression. The same rule applies to any future event an institution funds,
however the till records it.
